===== K8s Task 5.1: the last-applied-configuration leak =====
Applying a Secret with 'kubectl apply' stores the ENTIRE object, password
included, in a plaintext annotation on the live resource:
  annotation contains keys: ['POSTGRES_DB', 'POSTGRES_PASSWORD', 'POSTGRES_USER']
  POSTGRES_PASSWORD present in the annotation: True

  -> anyone with 'get secret' (or even 'describe') sees it; 'kubectl apply'
     is the wrong verb for secrets. Use 'kubectl create secret ... --dry-run
     | kubectl replace', or a tool that never writes the annotation.
